Why are we building RashtraLink?

For years, India’s public square has been shaped by black-box algorithms designed thousands of miles away in Silicon Valley. Systems engineered to amplify outrage, maximize polarization, and trap minds in endless dopamine loops—all to sell ad impressions.

India is home to 1.4 billion citizens with brilliant researchers, creators, thinkers, and builders. Our intellectual soul and national conversation cannot be rented out to foreign recommendation feeds.

Our Three Guiding Foundations:

1. Transparent Algorithmic Control: You govern what enters your feed with simple, visible priority levers. No shadowbanning, no hidden manipulation.

2. The Charcha Arena: Moving past toxic comment wars into structured, evidence-backed civil debates where citations and truth matter.

3. Indian Data Sovereignty: Built in strict alignment with the DPDP Act 2023. Your personal data stays on Indian soil, guarded by Indian law.

We are not building another frivolous feed. We are building India's sovereign attention and social networking platform—a true digital town square for our civilizational future. If this mission speaks to you, I warmly invite you to join our early access cohort and shape this with us.
